___IN TRIPURA___

Tripura's culture is a vibrant mix of Bengali and diverse indigenous tribal traditions, characterized by rich folk dances (Garia, Bizu, Hojagiri), music, and festivals like Kharchi and Garia Puja. The state is known for its cane, bamboo, and handicrafts, making it a beautiful blend of many cultural threads.
