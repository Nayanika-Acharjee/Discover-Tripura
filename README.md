# Discover-Tripura

🟣Tripura Tourism Website 🌟

Inspired by the grandeur of Incredible India, this website is a vibrant digital portal celebrating the hidden gems of Tripura. Built with React, GSAP, SVG animations, and Vanilla JS, it offers an immersive experience to explore the state's rich heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and warm hospitality.

⚡About Tripura
Tripura, a mesmerizing state in Northeast India, is a blend of natural beauty, cultural diversity, and historical intrigue. From the mystical hills of Jampui 🗻 to the serene waters of Rudrasagar Lake 🌊, this website invites travelers to uncover Tripura’s untold stories.

✨Features
- Responsive Design 📱: Seamlessly adapts to desktops, tablets, and mobiles.
- Dynamic Animations ✨: GSAP-powered transitions and scroll-triggered effects for a cinematic feel.
- SVG Illustrations 🎨: Handcrafted visuals highlighting Tripura’s landmarks and motifs.
- Interactive Elements 🔍: Vanilla JS-driven maps, galleries, and sliders for an engaging journey.
- Explore Tripura:
    - Top Attractions 🏯: Dive into Ujjayanta Palace, Neermahal, and the lush Jampui Hills.
    - Culture & Festivals 🎉: Experience Gajendra Narayan’s legacy, Kharchi Puja, and local crafts.
    - Travel Essentials ✈: Get quick links to plan your visit, accommodations, and local cuisine.

💡Highlights
- Hero Section 🌄: A sweeping animation of Tripura’s landscapes with a call-to-action.
- Destination Showcase 📸: Curated galleries with stories of each spot.
- Interactive Map 🗺: Explore locations with clickable pins.
- Contact & Plan 📬: A simple form to connect, plus links to local guides.

📜Tech Stack
- Frontend: React.js for a smooth, component-driven UI.
- Animations: GSAP for scroll-based storytelling.
- Graphics: Custom SVGs for a unique touch.
- Interactivity: Vanilla JavaScript for lightweight features.
- Styling: CSS3 with modern layouts.
